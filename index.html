About Me

Hey there! I'm Misokatsu, but you can call me Miso, Hashi, or Sleps!

I do digital art, simple graphic designs, and video editing on the side. I'm still learning and leveling up my design skills, so taking commissions is a super fun way for me to practice while drawing cool stuff for you guys!


Stuff I don't draw:
  • Furries, Mechas & Heavy Armor: Simple animal ears/tails are totally fine, but complex mecha or full furry characters are out of my comfort zone for now.
  • Overly Complex Designs: Super cluttered outfits or insane accessories without clear references.
  • Heavy Gore: Extreme violence or heavy bloodiness.
  • NSFW & Kinks: Not accepting NSFW or fetish art unless discussed beforehand for a much higher rate.
